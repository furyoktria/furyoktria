Our purpose

We make daily care that Indonesia can afford and the next generation can inherit.

Founded inside a pandemic to fix a single problem — single‑use masks choking our coastlines — Maskit has grown into a multi‑category FMCG building gentler products at fair prices, with materials that come from, and return to, the earth.

How we make things

Three non‑negotiables behind every Maskit pack.

01

Medical‑grade as the floor, not the ceiling.

Every product, mask or otherwise, is built in an ISO 13485 facility and licensed by Kementerian Kesehatan. The bar starts where most brands finish.

02

Plants in, plastics out.

Surfactants from coconut and palm. Mask layers that biodegrade. Bottles that refill. We engineer products to leave less behind than they bring.

03

An Indonesian price, always.

Our pricing ladder starts at Rp 7.000. Eco‑friendly should not be a luxury — it is the only standard we are willing to ship.

Planet & People · 2030
